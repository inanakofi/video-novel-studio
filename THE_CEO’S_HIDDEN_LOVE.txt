THE CEO’S HIDDEN LOVE
Genre: African romance · Drama · Betrayal · Social class

The central story
Kwame Asare is a 32-year-old millionaire CEO who owns one of Ghana's fastest-growing real estate companies. Handsome, disciplined, and emotionally guarded, he believes everybody has a price.
Akosua Mensah is a 25-year-old struggling young woman who sells waakye with her mother near Osu. She has little money, but she refuses to sacrifice her dignity for anyone.
One rainy evening, Akosua unknowingly helps a stranger who has just been robbed. She gives him her last money for transport, unaware that the man is one of Ghana's wealthiest bachelors.
When they meet again, Kwame offers her a job. Their attraction grows, but his wealthy family has already chosen a bride for him. Worse, someone from Akosua's past knows a secret that could destroy everything.
The twist: Akosua may be connected to the very family that ruined Kwame's childhood.
