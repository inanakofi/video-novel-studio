# Video Novel Studio 1.1 — editing and production upgrade

Implemented and verified locally on 11 October 2026. No additional Python
dependencies, cloud services or online inference were introduced.

## Available now

| Improvement | Where to use it | Behaviour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Precise caption editing | Timeline / captions | Real narration waveform, audition, seeking, draggable cue edges/centres, and start/end at playhead controls; manual SRT persists. |
| Visual editing timeline | Timeline / captions | Scene, transition, narration, music, SFX and caption tracks. Scene trims move subsequent captions/effects; invalid edits are rejected. Last rendered video plays alongside the timeline. |
| Series continuity | Series → Continuity tracker; scene editor; Writing | User-authored character/location records, scene links, open/resolved threads with episode references, previous endings and relevant continuity context. |
| Reusable styles | Series → Series style | Fonts, colours, card templates, narrator choice, music and levels. New episodes inherit defaults; Apply updates an existing episode explicitly. |
| Faster rerenders | Automatic; CLI `--no-cache` to bypass | Reuse unchanged scene encodes; rebuild changed shots/dependent transitions. Content/checksum validation, bounded shot cache and normal final-export verification. |
| Publishing check | Publishing; `python -m src.publishing …` | Asset/timing errors and advisory duration, caption readability/margin and narration-pause findings. Timed findings jump to the timeline. |

Auto captions now enforce two wrapped lines for ordinary words and stay in sync
with the renderer when the project is saved. Very long single words can still
need manual shortening. Existing recordings are never silently regenerated by
applying a voice default. Original Markdown bibles remain editable alongside the
new structured continuity records.

**46 automated tests passed in 50.706 seconds**, with `VNS_GUI_TESTS=1`.
This includes the original media/desktop suite and new tests for actual mouse
drags, caption persistence, scene ripple edits, audio boundaries, continuous-track
protection, transitions, sound-effect placement, real waveform decoding/audition,
series inheritance, continuity validation, caption wrapping, publishing findings,
cache reuse/invalidation and recovery from deliberately damaged cache entries.

The cache test renders actual MP4s: a caption-only change reuses all five shots;
replacing the first illustration rebuilds that scene and the next crossfade,
while the remaining shots are reused. Damaged cache files are rebuilt. Existing
cancellation, publication rollback and safe rerun tests continue to pass.

The delivered [updated sample MP4](exports/the_letter_at_the_gate_episode_001.mp4)
is **75 seconds, 1080 × 1920, 30 FPS, H.264/AAC**. Audio ends at 75.000 seconds;
18 captions end by 69.410 seconds. FFprobe checks and a complete FFmpeg decode

Actual desktop playback was checked at 10, 34, 60 and 74 seconds, with 58 decoded

## Practical limits and operation

- Caption generation is proportional timing. Listen and use the waveform editor
  for precise synchronization; no speech recognition model was added.
- The timeline's video panel shows the last actual export. Rerender to see edits.
  Narration audition shows still artwork/current text, without claiming live
  composited video. Reload the waveform after changing audio or scene duration.
- Ripple trims require separate scene recordings. Continuous episode recordings
  must first be split or edited as audio to preserve synchronization.
- Publishing warnings use editorial heuristics. Overlay guides do not guarantee
  compatibility with every platform UI, and pause detection does not understand
  dialogue or dramatic intent.
- Shot encodes are limited to approximately 1 GiB per episode. Waveform files are
  also derived local data. Remove an episode's `.vns-cache/` only while playback
  and rendering are stopped if disk space needs reclaiming.

Open the app with `python -m src.app` from the project directory, or run
`Start-Studio.ps1`. Full operating instructions are in [README.md](README.md).
